# Final Identity Saturation-Stop Audit

## Decision

Identity divergence stops at 2,310 raw observations and 2,052 normalized
candidate entities. The stop is based on marginal coverage gain, not a desired
shortlist size. No candidate is rejected, ranked, selected, or recommended by
this audit.

The frozen corpus contains 36 schema-valid, workflow-declared portfolios,
2,346 lossless registry events, 258 preserved duplicate occurrences, and 462
wildcard occurrences. Every declared generation floor passes.

## Semantic Stop Test

The two independent 100-observation semantic challenge portfolios used
different prompts, methods, and payload hashes. Together they contain 199
distinct surfaces; `Pantry` is the only surface recurrence.

The second portfolio reproduced all ten challenged semantic regions but added
no new region and no new atlas concept. It corrected unsupported statements in
the earlier portfolio and retained dissenting or awkward observations rather
than replacing them. Another model generation wave would increase examples,
not semantic territory coverage. Human, community, accessibility, and legal
review cannot be supplied by another model generation wave.

## Form Stop Test

Four independent form challenge waves produced 400 observations across 16
portfolios. Before closure they contained 372 normalized entities and 28
preserved duplicate occurrences. The audit found broad coverage of derivation,
blending, conversion, compact forms, code tokens, counter-claims, source
language probes, citation architectures, and identity systems.

Only two literal mechanism floors remained: ideophones and deliberate
reduplication. A provider-diverse ten-observation closure added seven
non-wildcard ideophones, including two reduplicated forms, one non-wildcard
control, and two explicit wildcards. All ten surfaces were new to the accepted
corpus. The closure therefore resolves the measured deficit without opening a
new broad form wave.

## Downstream Evidence

Every normalized entity has deterministic code, speech, prose, architecture,
and international-screen records. The international screen reports machine
signals and limitations only: 1,556 candidates require attention, all 2,052
retain at least one unverified dimension, and zero have human validation.

Exact package-record checks are complete for Hex, npm, PyPI, and crates.io:
8,208 current candidate/source checks, zero unresolved checks, and 1,682
observed exact-record flags. These observations are not availability,
trademark, marketplace, or legal clearance.

## Stop Boundaries

This stop establishes coverage saturation for the recorded semantic and form
mechanisms. It does not establish cultural acceptance, native-speaker review,
assistive-technology usability, legal clearance, market preference, or a final
identity. UTS #39 confusable screening and idempotent enrichment rebuilding are
tracked reusable follow-ups; their absence remains visible rather than being
converted into a safe claim.

The next checkpoint is evaluation of every entity by the declared independent
model profiles, followed by transparent scenario, tier, Pareto, wildcard,
dissent, resurrection, and hostile-audit views. The owner's hidden candidate
enters only after that checkpoint is committed, and `selection_made` remains
`false`.
