# Master-Brand Ranking

## Decision State

`DSX` remains the leading benchmark. It is the only current candidate that
compresses the exact thesis, **Declarative Self-improving Elixir**, while
preserving the project's DSPy lineage. Seven alternatives are strong enough to
challenge it before availability and speech screening: `Attune`, `Enact`,
`Accord`, `Praxon`, `Transduce`, `Declare`, and `Axima`.

No name has been selected. The owner's held-out candidate has not been seen,
and none of the taste-wave names has received current package, repository,
domain, trademark, international, or listener screening.

The central decision is architectural, not merely lexical:

- `DSX` is a lineage-bearing identity for the Elixir implementation;
- a name such as `Enact`, `Transduce`, or `Attune` could instead become a
  runtime-neutral umbrella, with the Elixir lineage expressed below it.

## How To Read The Ranking

The complete frontier contains 220 names: `DSX`, 147 names from the owner's
positive-unlabeled pass, and all 72 taste-conditioned names. No generated name
was removed before ranking.

The synthesis considers owner-taste fit, semantic truth, umbrella durability,
spoken and code identity, and audience or connotation risk. It combines three
independent reviews focused on taste, project architecture, and adversarial
audiences with a maintainer synthesis. The top 40 are ordered. Lower bands are
alphabetized because distinctions at that resolution are not decision-useful.
They remain available for owner rescue and narrower roles.
