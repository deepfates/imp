# Land useful GRPO learning

Turn the demonstrated GRPO weight-update and artifact machinery into one useful ordinary learning result rather than treating tensor motion as completion.

## Acceptance Criteria

A bounded natural GRPO treatment produces valid sampled rollouts and non-uniform rewards, selects a trained artifact on separate validation data, improves untouched utility over base, and content-verifies/rebinds for fresh inference. If the shipped backend or default treatment cannot do this after falsification, narrow its public claim explicitly instead of calling mechanics a successful optimizer.
